1. Purpose & Executive Statement of Intent / Inhloso Yesivumelwano

English Version [EN]

This Letter of Intent (LOI) confirms the mutual agreement between Lwandile Engineering Projects (Pty) Ltd (led by Sipho Wiseman Khuzwayo) in partnership with the Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy (led by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure) and the eMbalenhle Disability Community Leadership (represented by Mduduzi Wiseman Nkosi and Sifiso Shongwe).

Inguqulo yesiZulu [ZU]

Le ncwadi yenhloso (LOI) iqinisekisa ukuvunyelwa ngokubambisana phakathi kwe-Lwandile Engineering Projects (eholwa uSipho Wiseman Khuzwayo) ngokubambisana ne-Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy (eholwa uDr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure) kanye nabaholi Bomphakathi Ophila Ngokukhubazeka waseMbalenhle (bemelwe uMduduzi Wiseman Nkosi noS'fiso Shongwe).

THE IRRESISTABLE PARTNERSHIP PROPOSITION / ISIPHAKAMISO SOKUBAMBISANA

This agreement guarantees that the 20,000 m² (2-hectare) eMbalenhle site will anchor the Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus, empowering persons with disabilities through community equity participation, accessible infrastructure, electric wheelchair charging hubs, and priority access to green-technology training, employment, and enterprise incubation. Solar and biogas will support energy and heat generation, alongside piggery and poultry facilities, fostering inclusive, sustainable local economic development and skills transfer. The campus will serve as a model for disability-inclusive innovation and entrepreneurship, ensuring that the local community benefits directly from the Just Energy Transition (JET) funding and associated socio-economic transformation initiatives.

Lesi sivumelwano siqinisekisa ukuthi umhlaba wama-20,000 m² (AmaHektha angu-2) okuyingxenye yomhlaba owabelwe umphkathi ophila ngokukhubazeka waseMbalenhle, usetshenziselwe ukusungula iSikhungo se-Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus. Lesisivumelwano siqinisekisa ukuthuthukiswa kwamakhono, namathuba emisebenzi ikakhulukazi emphakathini ophila ngokukhubazeka. lengqalasizinda izoba isisekelo sokuthuthukiswa komnotho wendawo. Leprojhekthi izoba namahhoko amabili okukhulisa izingulube kanye nezinkukhu, futhi ibe neziteshi zokushaja izihlalo ezinamasondo zikagesi (mobility wheelchairs), ugesi nokushisa siyaziphehlela ngokusebenzisa i-solar ngokubambisana ne-Biogas. Umphakathi ophila ngokukhubazeka ubekwa kuqala ezinhlelweni zokuqeqeshwa kwamabhizinisi aluhlaza (self-sustanaible green innoations), ngosizo lwezimali labakwa Just Energy Transition (JET) kanye nezinye izinhlelo zokuguqulwa komnotho emphakathini.

2. Key Partnership Terms & Land Allocation / Imigomo Ebalulekile Yokubambisana

Partnership Term (EN) / Umgomo (ZU) Specific Commitment & Technical Detail Beneficiary Impact / Umthelela Kumphakathi
Land Footprint & Location 20,000 m² (2.0 Hectares) leasehold site located in eMbalenhle, Govan Mbeki Municipality. Secures prime site for Phase 1 MVP deployment (ZAR 3.531M CAPEX).
Ownership & Lease Model Long-term 20-Year Renewable Leasehold with a structured Community Dividend Share. Sustainable, recurring rental and enterprise revenue for local community funds.
Zone M Mobility Infrastructure Construction of a fully accessible Accessibility Hub including 4× Wheelchair Charging Bays & 30 kW DC EV Charger. Free, 100% off-grid solar charging for electric wheelchairs and mobility scooters.
Preferential Employment & Training Minimum 30% direct placement quota for persons with disabilities in SCADA monitoring, administration, and campus operations. Direct human capital development via the Inclusive Entrepreneurship Academy.
JET EOI Strategic Weight Full inclusion of disability leadership within the official Just Energy Transition funding submission. High-priority scoring for social inclusion and socio-economic transformation.

3. Mutual Obligations of the Parties / Izibopho Ezihlanganyelwe

3.1 Obligations of Lwandile Engineering & Inclusive Entrepreneurship Academy
  • Full capital raising and technical execution under the Scapush Precision Execution Standard.
  • Construction of 100% step-free, universally accessible pathways across all 20,000 m² of campus footprint.
  • Provision of accredited green skills training modules (Solar, SCADA, Livestock, Biogas) tailored for persons with disabilities.
  • Installation of clean water points and zero-diesel solar power hubs for local community benefit.
3.2 Obligations of eMbalenhle Disability Community Leadership
  • Guaranteeing undisputed land availability and support for site access during environmental and civil works.
  • Active participation in the Campus Governance Board to oversee community integration and equity distribution.
  • Mobilizing community candidates for operator certification and green enterprise incubation programs.
