Lwandile Engineering Projects, in strategic partnership with the Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy (founded by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure), formally submits this Expression of Interest (EOI) under Window 4 of the Just Energy Transition (JET) Funding Platform. 4. Key Focus Areas & Technical Scope

4.1 Climate-Smart & Controlled-Environment Agriculture (Hydroponics & Agrivoltaics)
  • Agrivoltaic Integration: Dual-use land development deploying elevated solar PV structures over active crop production areas, optimizing land efficiency and providing microclimate shading.
  • Controlled-Environment Hydroponics: Closed-loop nutrient film technique (NFT) and deep water culture (DWC) systems reducing water usage by up to 90% relative to traditional open-field farming.
  • Resilient Crop Selection: Prioritizing high-value leafy greens, tomatoes, and regional staple crops tailored for local supply chains and municipal produce markets.
4.2 Agrifood-Related Organic Waste-to-Value (Composting & Biogas)
  • Anaerobic Digestion (Biogas): Capture and conversion of agricultural residues and food waste into clean biogas for farm-level thermal and electrical energy requirements.
  • Commercial Composting: Processing digestate and organic bypass streams into high-grade organic bio-fertilizers to rehabilitate degraded soil in surrounding farming communities.
  • Circular Resource Loops: Complete recovery of water, nutrients, and energy within the farm boundary to minimize external inputs and operational expenditure.
4.3 Governance, Skills Transfer & Institutional Capacity Building
  • Inclusive Entrepreneurial Academy Modules: Structured capacity-building and business incubators designed by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure to build sustainable local green enterprises.
  • Accredited Technical Training: Comprehensive workforce upskilling covering renewable energy maintenance, anaerobic digester management, and hydroponic operations.
  • Community Food Hubs & Outgrowers: Establishing localized aggregation centers and providing certified outgrower support to integrate smallholders into commercial value chains.
