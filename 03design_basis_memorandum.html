1. Document Purpose & Project Identity

Document Purpose

This Design Basis Memorandum establishes the technical, functional, operational and strategic foundation of the Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus. The DBM defines what the facility is intended to achieve, the assumptions under which it shall operate, the engineering philosophy that guides its design, and the criteria against which all future design work shall be measured.

2. Strategic Vision, Mission & Core Objectives

Vision Statement

To establish a self-sustaining, disability-inclusive, closed-loop food-energy-water nexus capable of demonstrating how renewable energy, biological systems, digital intelligence and community participation can be integrated into a single regenerative ecosystem.

Mission Statement

To design, construct, operate and continuously improve a Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus capable of producing food, energy, skills, knowledge and economic opportunity while minimising waste, dependency and environmental impact.

Primary Strategic Objectives
  • Food production sovereignty
  • Renewable energy sovereignty
  • Water security and resilience
  • Biosecurity leadership
  • Accessibility leadership
  • Inclusive entrepreneurship development
  • Digital monitoring and optimisation
  • Demonstration of circular economy principles
  • Rural and township technology validation
  • Just Energy Transition alignment

3. Design Philosophy & Site Parameters

Core Philosophy: Food + Energy + Water + Knowledge

The campus shall not consist of isolated systems. Each subsystem shall contribute to and support the others. Food systems generate biological feedstock. Feedstock generates biogas. Biogas generates energy and thermal recovery. Energy powers production systems. Production generates learning opportunities. Learning generates entrepreneurship. Entrepreneurship supports community prosperity.

4. Subsystem Design Basis & Systems Integration

Energy Philosophy & Priority

Off-Grid Sovereignty: Independent of utility grid power.

Prohibited Source: Diesel generators shall form NO part of the design basis.

Primary Sources: Solar PV | Biogas CHP | Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)

Energy Dispatch Priority:

  1. Solar Generation
  2. Battery Energy Storage
  3. Biogas CHP Support
Water Philosophy

Water security is critically important to operational continuity. The design is based upon:

  • Borehole abstraction
  • Water treatment & filtration
  • Elevated storage tanks
  • Gravity distribution grid
  • Continuous quality & flow monitoring
Livestock Production Design Basis
Poultry System:
  • 4,800 Broiler Chicks
  • 1 Poultry Barn (12m x 32m)
  • 4 Biosecure Pods
  • 1,200 Head Capacity
  • Microclimate Nursery Infrastructure
  • Manure Resource Recovery System
  • Direct Biogas Feedstock Contribution
Piggery System:
  • 160 Weaners
  • 1 Piggery Barn (12m x 32m)
  • 8 Biosecure Pods
  • 20 Head Capacity
  • Microclimate Nursery Infrastructure
  • Manure Resource Recovery System
  • Direct Biogas Feedstock Contribution

5. Biosecurity, Accessibility, Monitoring & Future Expansion

Layered Biosecurity Philosophy
  1. Perimeter Security
  2. Visitor Control
  3. Vehicle Screening
  4. Personnel Screening
  5. Shower Transition
  6. Pod Isolation
  7. Thermal Firebreaks
  8. Emergency Containment Protocols
Accessibility & Central SCADA Monitoring
Accessibility Design Principles:
  • Accessible routes, learning spaces & administration
  • Accessible sanitation & mobility device charging
  • Accessible EV charging infrastructure
Centralized SCADA Monitoring:

Digitally monitors Energy, Water, Livestock, Biodigester, CHP, Solar, Battery, Environmental, and Security Systems.

Future Expansion Philosophy
