BIOTIC CIRCULARITY PRINCIPLE (100% WASTE RE-UTILIZATION)

Organic excreta and washdown slurry from the 4,800 broilers and 160 swine are gravity-fed into the 70m³ mesophilic anaerobic biodigester located securely within the 20,000m² campus boundary. This biological conversion yields approximately 21.6 m³ of high-purity biogas per day (fueling the 15 kW CHP engine) and 3.3 m³ of pathogen-free organic liquid digestate per day, which serves as nutrient-rich bio-fertilizer for campus agriculture.
