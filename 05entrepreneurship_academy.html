1. Purpose, Institutional Vision & Strategic Objectives

The Inclusive Entrepreneurship Academy functions as the education, training, skills development, human capital transformation, and enterprise incubation centre of the Sovereign Bio-Solar-Thermal Campus. Led by Dr. Palesa Charlotte Felix-Faure, the Academy bridges technical engineering, circular agriculture, and off-grid utilities with accredited human skills development and enterprise creation under the Just Energy Transition (JET) framework.

INSTITUTIONAL VISION & INCLUSION MANDATE

To serve as a community-centred centre of excellence for Food, Energy, Water, Circular Economy, and Accessibility Innovation. The Academy guarantees step-free, fully accessible pathways for youth, women, and persons with disabilities, ensuring complete economic and technical inclusion within South Africa's green transition.

Core Strategic Objectives
  • Green Skills Development: Building local technical competence across solar PV, microgrids, biodigesters, and CHP.
  • Disability & Youth Inclusion: Tailored learning tracks guaranteeing 100% universal access for all abilities.
  • Enterprise Incubation: Transforming technical competencies into viable commercial cooperatives and green SMEs.
  • Food-Energy-Water Literacy: Educating community leaders on sovereign, zero-waste circular ecosystems.
  • Employment Readiness: Certifying operators for direct deployment across off-grid infrastructure assets.
Academy 3-Tier Learning Framework
Learning Tier Category Standard Target Audience & Delivery Purpose
Tier 1 Accredited Qualification Pathways Formal NQF Level 2 to 5 occupational qualifications and credit-bearing skills programmes.

Tier 3 Community Awareness & Inclusion Broad community workshops, food-energy-water security literacy, and disability inclusion.

Quick Reference Operational Refresher Modules

10-minute digital and physical cheat-sheets stationed at equipment control panels for rapid field execution:

  • Daily Poultry Operations: Morning feeder checks, temperature verification, water meter reading.
  • Daily Swine Operations: Slurry channel flushing, feed hopper inspection, pen health score.
  • Biosecurity Procedures: Shower-in/shower-out sequence, vehicle arch disinfectant mix.
  • SCADA Alarm Response: Priority 1 emergency clearing, operator override, emergency contact.
  • Borehole & Water Systems: Pump manual start, UV filter lamp check, tank valve switching.
  • Biodigester Start-Up: Macerator unblock procedure, pH testing, gas valve line alignment.
  • CHP Engine Start-Up: Pre-start oil level, gas pressure check, synchronizing breaker closing.
  • BESS Emergency Procedures: Thermal runaway alarm response, manual isolator trip, HVAC check.
  • Microgrid Black Start: BESS control energization, 400V bus build, Tier 1 load closing.
  • Emergency Response Procedures: Campus evacuation routes, assembly point headcount.
  • Accessibility Support: Wheelchair charger deployment, mobility scooter assist, ramp check.
